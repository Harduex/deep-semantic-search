Computer vision is the field of artificial intelligence that enables machines to
interpret and understand visual information from the world. It encompasses a wide
range of tasks including image classification, object detection, semantic segmentation,
and image generation.

CLIP (Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training) by OpenAI is a multimodal model that
learns visual concepts from natural language supervision. It can match images with
text descriptions, enabling zero-shot image classification and cross-modal search.

BLIP (Bootstrapping Language-Image Pre-training) is a vision-language model that
excels at image captioning and visual question answering. It generates natural
language descriptions of images by combining visual encoders with language decoders.

Image clustering groups visually similar images together using techniques like
KMeans on feature vectors extracted by models such as CLIP. This enables automatic
organization of large image collections without manual labeling.
