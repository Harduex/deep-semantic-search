Deep learning is a subset of machine learning that uses neural networks with many layers.
These deep neural networks are capable of learning complex patterns from large amounts of data.
Common architectures include convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image recognition,
recurrent neural networks (RNNs) for sequential data, and transformers for natural language processing.

The key advantage of deep learning is its ability to automatically discover useful feature
representations from raw data, eliminating the need for manual feature engineering.
Training deep networks requires substantial computational resources, often leveraging GPUs
for parallel matrix computations.

Popular frameworks for deep learning include PyTorch, TensorFlow, and JAX.
Transfer learning allows practitioners to fine-tune pre-trained models on new tasks
with relatively small datasets, dramatically reducing training time and data requirements.
